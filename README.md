# Spider Man 3
### *
Plot Overview
- Peter and MJ's Struggles: Peter plans to propose to Mary Jane, who has just debuted on Broadway, while enjoying public adoration in New York City. However, their relationship strains as Peter grows overconfident and distracted.
- Harry Osborn's Vengeance: Harry, blaming Peter for his father's death, attacks him using Green Goblin tech before suffering partial amnesia. He later regains his memory and schemes against Peter.
- The Rise of Sandman: Escaped convict Flint Marko falls into a particle accelerator during a police chase, fusing his body with sand and transforming into the shape-shifting Sandman. It is revealed that Marko—not just an ordinary thief—was the actual gunman who killed Uncle Ben.
- The Black Symbiote Suit: An extraterrestrial alien symbiote attaches to Peter's moped and bonds with his suit, enhancing his physical abilities but corrupting his personality into an aggressive, arrogant, and vengeful individual.
- Venom and the Final Battle: After Peter sheds the alien suit due to its destructive influence, it bonds with disgraced rival photographer Eddie Brock, creating Venom. Sandman and Venom join forces against Peter, leading to Harry sacrificing himself to help save his friend. Peter eventually defeats Venom and forgives Flint Marko.*

## Summary
Peter Parker finally achieves a balance between his life as Spider-Man and his relationship with Mary Jane Watson. However, his world is turned upside down when a mysterious alien symbiote attaches to his suit, turning it black and amplifying his dark, aggressive impulses. This internal struggle coincides with the arrival of two dangerous new villains: Flint Marko, who transforms into the Sandman, and Edward Brock Jr., a rival photographer who becomes the monstrous Venom. Additionally, Peter faces a deeply personal threat from his former best friend, Harry Osborn, who seeks revenge for his father's death by adopting the mantle of the New Goblin. Ultimately, Peter must battle his own inner demons and join forces with unexpected allies to save the people he loves from the overwhelming alliance of his enemies.

---

## Important Characters of the Movie

 1. **Peter Parker / Spider-Man (Tobey Maguire):** The central hero who struggles with pride, anger, and moral forgiveness after bonding with an alien symbiote while dealing with fractured relationships.
 
 2. **Mary Jane Watson (Kirsten Dunst):** Peter’s girlfriend and Broadway aspiring actress whose relationship with Peter hits a rocky patch due to his growing arrogance and emotional distance.

 3. **Harry Osborn / New Goblin (James Franco):** Peter's former best friend who seeks vengeance for his father's death by taking up the mantle of the Goblin.

 4. **Flint Marko / Sandman (Thomas Haden Church):** A fugitive petty criminal who gains shapeshifting sand powers and is revealed to have a tragic personal connection to Uncle Ben’s death.

 5. **Eddie Brock / Venom (Topher Grace):** Peter's professional rival at the Daily Bugle who harbors deep resentment and merges with the discarded alien symbiote to become Venom.
